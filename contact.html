Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment to visit the clinic? +

Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend booking an appointment online to avoid wait times, especially for specialist consultations.

What insurance plans do you accept? +

We work with most major insurance providers. Contact our front desk with your provider's name and we'll confirm coverage before your visit.

Can I get a video consultation instead of visiting in person? +

Yes — select "Videocall" when booking, and one of our doctors will connect with you at your scheduled time.

How soon will I hear back after submitting the contact form? +

Our team typically responds within 24 hours, Monday through Saturday.
